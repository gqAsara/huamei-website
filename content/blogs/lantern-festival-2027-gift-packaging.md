# Lantern Festival 2027 gift packaging: red gift sets, CNY extended gifting, and production briefing

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 11 August 2026. Updated 11 August 2026.

Sonia Sun has produced Chinese New Year and Lantern Festival packaging programmes at Huamei since 1992, supplying rigid gift boxes for spirits, confectionery, tea, and wellness brands across the full extended CNY season — from the first gifts that move in late January through to Lantern Festival on the 15th day of the first lunar month.

Most international buyers treat the Chinese New Year gifting season as a single event with a single briefing window. The brands that have been producing in the Chinese market for more than a few seasons know it as two events: the main CNY peak, and the Lantern Festival tail. Packaging designed for the main peak can sell through on the first two days of the holiday; packaging designed to extend through the Lantern Festival uses a distinct visual register and is frequently a smaller, more focused gift format than the main CNY launch.

## When is Lantern Festival 2027 and how does it differ from Chinese New Year for gift packaging?

Lantern Festival 2027 (元宵节, Yuánxiāo Jié) falls on 12 February 2027 — the 15th day of the first lunar month, exactly 15 days after Chinese New Year on 28 January 2027. For gift packaging, Lantern Festival extends the CNY gifting season by two weeks, with demand concentrated on smaller gift sets, sweet food packaging, and lantern-motif designs in red and gold.

The core product category associated with Lantern Festival is tangyuan (汤圆) — sweet glutinous rice balls — and other sweet foods consumed at family reunions on the festival evening. Premium food gift packaging for Lantern Festival is therefore a distinct SKU from the main CNY gift box: smaller footprint, typically a two-to-four item format, with a design register that references lanterns, moon imagery, and warmer red-gold colour combinations rather than the dragon and phoenix motifs common in CNY packaging.

"Lantern Festival 2027 falls on 12 February — 15 days after CNY on 28 January 2027. For packaging purposes, it creates a distinct demand window for smaller gift sets, sweet food presentation boxes, and extended-season limited editions in a lantern and moon design register."

## What packaging structures work for Lantern Festival 2027 gift sets?

Lantern Festival gift sets cluster around three structure types: two-piece lift-off rigid boxes for premium sweet food sets, magnetic closure boxes for cosmetic and wellness gift sets, and sleeve-and-tray formats for confectionery and tea.

The two-piece lift-off structure in the 200–350 mm footprint range — built from 2.0–2.5 mm greyboard with a velvet or foam insert — is the primary structure for premium tangyuan and confectionery gift sets. The structure is simple, cost-effective at 200+ pieces MOQ, and prints and foils cleanly. A 128 gsm red wrapping paper with gold [hot-foil](/craft/hot-foil) stamping and a blind [emboss](/craft/emboss) of the lantern or moon motif is the typical surface treatment.

Magnetic closure boxes in the 150–200 mm footprint range work for cosmetic and personal care gift sets — skin care pairs, fragrance sets, or health supplement collections positioned as Lantern Festival limited editions. The magnetic closure box communicates value at price points above CNY 300; the gift receiver often repurposes the box, which extends the brand presence past the gifting moment.

Huamei's magnetic closure pull-force is held at 6–50 g at 2,800 Gauss across closure types and board weights, ensuring a satisfying close regardless of the box footprint.

## How should Lantern Festival packaging differ visually from main CNY packaging?

Lantern Festival packaging uses a warmer, more intimate visual register than main CNY packaging — lantern shapes, circular moon motifs, and warm red-amber colour combinations rather than the large-format dragon, phoenix, and auspicious character motifs of the main season.

The distinction serves a commercial purpose: a brand that releases main CNY packaging in late January and a distinct Lantern Festival edition in early February maintains shelf presence and gifting relevance through the full 15-day holiday period. Department stores and premium retailers in the Chinese market actively rotate their gifting floors over the CNY golden week; a second, visually distinct presentation keeps the brand visible for the Lantern Festival peak.

The hot-foil palette for Lantern Festival packaging differs slightly from main CNY: warm antique gold rather than bright mirror gold, with occasional rose-gold accents for cosmetic programmes. Huamei holds seventeen curated foil colours in-house; the antique gold and warm copper tones suited to Lantern Festival design are in the standard in-house range and do not require special ordering.

"Lantern Festival packaging uses lantern and moon motifs with a warm antique-gold foil palette rather than the bright mirror gold and large-format auspicious characters of main CNY packaging. The visual distinction maintains brand shelf presence through the full extended gifting season."

## What is the production calendar for Lantern Festival 2027 packaging from China?

Lantern Festival 2027 production must complete before the Chinese New Year factory closure — which begins around 25 January 2027 — leaving a hard cut-off that places the brief deadline in November 2026, not January.

Huamei factories close for the CNY holiday period, typically from a few days before CNY (28 January) through to approximately 8–14 February depending on the production schedule that year. This means production for Lantern Festival 2027 must complete before the factory closure, not after.

| Action | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Design brief confirmed, paper and foil references sent | 15 November 2026 |
| Sample approval (physical sample confirmed) | 1 December 2026 |
| Production run confirmed, PO released | 5 December 2026 |
| Production complete, goods shipped before CNY closure | 15–20 January 2027 |
| Goods arrive at destination before Lantern Festival | 25 January–5 February 2027 |

[Maersk](https://www.maersk.com/news/articles/2024/11/07/chinese-new-year-2025) and other international freight carriers publish annual CNY factory-closure and freight-capacity guidance in October–November; international buyers should consult their freight provider's CNY bulletin when confirming shipping windows for 2027.

Brands that brief a main CNY programme and a Lantern Festival extension simultaneously can share tooling between both programmes — the same die set, same structural specification, same board grade — with only the wrapping paper and insert design differing between the two SKUs. This reduces tooling cost and sample time for the second SKU.
