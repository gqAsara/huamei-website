# Packaging brief calendar for 2027 seasonal launches: when to order for CNY, Mid-Autumn, Christmas, Eid, and Diwali

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 4 August 2026. Updated 4 August 2026.

Sonia Sun has run seasonal packaging programmes at Huamei across four factories in Henan, Zhejiang, Sichuan, and Guizhou since founding the company in Zhengzhou in 1992 — including Chinese New Year gifting programmes for spirits and cosmetics brands that must ship to international destinations months before the holiday.

Seasonal packaging fails in one consistent way: the brief arrives too late. A brand that commissions Chinese New Year packaging in December for a February holiday has already missed the production window for an export programme. The calendar below sets out the correct brief deadlines for every major 2027 seasonal launch, working backwards from the holiday date through production, ocean freight transit, and in-store setup time. Use it to plan the full 2027 launch calendar now, while 2026 Q3 is still live.

## When should I brief a packaging manufacturer for Chinese New Year 2027?

> Chinese New Year 2027 falls on 17 February. To ship production by early January 2027 — allowing four to six weeks for ocean freight to a US or EU destination — production must complete by mid-December 2026. Factoring in 15–20 days of production and 7–10 days for sample approval, the packaging brief must be placed by mid-October 2026 at the latest. Brands requiring custom tooling or a new structure should brief by early October.

Chinese New Year (CNY) is the highest-stakes seasonal event in the luxury packaging calendar for spirits, cosmetics, and gifting brands with Chinese consumer audiences — whether domestically or in international markets with Chinese diaspora purchasing power. The gifting window runs from roughly two weeks before the holiday through the first week of the new year.

**CNY 2027 brief deadline: 10 October 2026.** This allows:
- 7–10 days for sample production and courier delivery to the buyer
- 5–7 days for buyer sample approval
- 15–20 days for full production run
- 5 days for export documentation and container loading
- 28–35 days ocean freight to the US West Coast (Los Angeles) or 32–40 days to European ports
- 7–10 days domestic distribution from port to distribution centre or retail

For domestic China distribution, the production completion deadline extends to early January 2027, and the brief deadline moves to mid-November 2026. For air-freight programmes where speed is prioritised over cost, the brief deadline extends to mid-November 2026 for ocean destinations as well.

"Huamei's CNY 2027 packaging brief deadline for ocean-freight export programmes to the US or EU is 10 October 2026. Brands with new tooling requirements should brief by 1 October 2026."

For context on the CNY structural options — drawer boxes, magnetic-closure cases, book-style gift sets — see [Chinese New Year gift packaging 2027](/blogs/chinese-new-year-gift-packaging-2027).

## When should I brief for Mid-Autumn Festival 2027?

Mid-Autumn Festival 2027 falls on 1 October. The gifting season opens approximately three weeks before the holiday — late September. Mooncake boxes, tea gift sets, and premium confectionery packaging are the dominant categories.

**Mid-Autumn 2027 brief deadline: 20 June 2027.** Timeline:
- 7–10 day sample window
- 7 days sample approval
- 20 days production
- 5 days export
- 32–40 days ocean freight to Europe or US East Coast
- 10 days domestic distribution

The Mid-Autumn season is compact — the gifting window is shorter than CNY, and late arrivals are more damaging because the holiday date is fixed and consumers purchase in a two-week window. Brief early to protect sample revision cycles. [DEEPURE's tea packaging](/volumes/t2-tea) — T2 True Brews at Huamei — illustrates the colour-coded seasonal sleeve format typical for tea gifting.

"For Mid-Autumn Festival 2027 packaging shipping by ocean freight to the US or EU, the brief deadline is 20 June 2027. The Mid-Autumn gifting window is two to three weeks — late arrivals are not recoverable."

## When should I brief for Christmas 2027?

Christmas 2027 falls on Saturday, 25 December. Retail in-store availability for Christmas gifting runs from 1 November through 24 December in the US and Europe. For brands targeting Black Friday and the peak gift-purchasing window (the two weeks before Christmas), in-store availability by 15 November is the target.

**Christmas 2027 brief deadline: 31 July 2027.** Timeline (for 15 November in-store):
- 10 day sample window
- 7 days sample approval
- 20 days production
- 5 days export
- 35 days ocean freight (US East Coast or Northern Europe)
- 14 days domestic distribution and retail setup

For US West Coast destinations, the ocean freight leg shortens by 7–10 days, pushing the brief deadline to 10 August 2027. For air-freight programmes, the brief deadline extends to mid-September 2027.

Christmas rigid box packaging — magnetic-closure cases for spirits, presentation cases for cosmetics, drawer boxes for confectionery gift sets — typically runs 500–3,000 units per SKU for premium brands. [Wuliangye's premium box programme](/volumes/wuliangye-68) illustrates the red-and-gold hot-foil register typical for premium gift packaging.

## When should I brief for Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Eid al-Fitr 2027 falls on 20 August (subject to moon sighting; confirm with the official lunar calendar). The gifting window opens approximately ten days before Eid and runs through the three-day holiday. Packaging categories include date boxes, fragrance gift sets, premium confectionery, and jewellery presentation cases.

**Eid 2027 brief deadline: 15 May 2027.** Timeline:
- 10 day sample window
- 7 days approval
- 20 days production
- 5 days export
- 20–25 days ocean freight to UAE/Saudi Arabia
- 10 days domestic distribution and retail setup

Eid packaging for the Gulf market has distinct decoration preferences: gold foil on white or ivory board, Arabic calligraphy or geometric lattice emboss, and structured internal trays for date or confectionery presentation. For the luxury Gulf market specifically, see [luxury packaging for the Gulf market](/blogs/luxury-packaging-gulf-market) for structural and decoration guidance.

"For Eid al-Fitr 2027 packaging reaching Gulf market retail by early August, the brief deadline is 15 May 2027, allowing for the full ocean freight, documentation, and retail distribution window."

## When should I brief for Diwali 2027?

Diwali 2027 falls on 26 October. The Diwali gifting season — sweets, dry fruits, premium home goods, and beauty gifts — runs from approximately 10 to 31 October in India and international Indian diaspora markets.

**Diwali 2027 brief deadline: 31 July 2027.** Timeline:
- 10 day sample window
- 7 days approval
- 20 days production
- 5 days export
- 22–28 days ocean freight to India (Mumbai or Chennai)
- 7 days inland distribution

For Indian domestic retail distribution, customs clearance time should be added as a buffer — typically 5–10 working days for packaged goods through Indian customs. Brief early to absorb delays in the customs clearance window.

## Tooling and new structure lead times

All of the deadlines above assume the structural format is already in Huamei's library (99 structures on file). For a new structure requiring custom tooling — a die, a bespoke insert mould, or a new structural format outside the standard library — add 10–15 working days to the production window. New tooling must be confirmed before sample production begins.

The practical implication: brands briefing a new structure for a seasonal launch should treat the tooling approval as a separate step from the sample brief. The structural file is approved, tooling is cut, and a structural sample is returned before the decoration sample brief is placed. This two-step sequence protects the decoration investment from structural revision.

"For seasonal packaging programmes with new structural tooling, add 10–15 working days to the brief deadline shown above. Tooling approval is the critical path — not the decoration sign-off."
