# Wig boxes with custom logo packaging: rigid box structures, branding options, and MOQ

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 5 June 2026. Updated 5 June 2026.

Sonia Sun has run custom rigid box production at Huamei's factories in Henan, Zhejiang, Sichuan, and Guizhou since founding the company in 1992, producing branded packaging for hair-care, beauty, and lifestyle brands shipping to US and European retail.

The wig and hair extension market has moved toward premium branded packaging over the past decade, with brands using the unboxing moment to reinforce perceived quality and justify retail price points. A wig box with a custom logo accomplishes two things at once: it protects the wig from compression, dust, and transit damage, and it becomes the brand's physical touchpoint at the moment of highest customer attention. This guide covers the structural formats, logo application methods, and MOQ realities for wig brands commissioning custom packaging from China.

## What is custom logo wig box packaging?

Custom logo wig box packaging is a rigid or folding box with printed or foil-stamped branding, sized to hold a wig or hairpiece in a satin or paper liner. Typical construction uses 1.5–2.5 mm greyboard with logo applied by hot-foil, screen print, or offset, at MOQ from 200 pieces.

The most common format is a lift-off lid or magnetic-flap rigid box in 2.0 mm greyboard, wrapped in a printed art paper and lined with a satin or soft-fabric insert. The insert holds the wig in position and prevents compression of the fibres during shipping. The rigid box construction — non-collapsible, set-up at the factory — is the standard because it provides consistent protection without assembly on the brand's side. For a full breakdown of rigid box construction, see [custom luxury rigid box manufacturing](/blogs/custom-luxury-rigid-box-manufacturing).

## What rigid box formats work best for wig packaging?

Lift-off lid rigid boxes are the most practical format for wig packaging because the full lid removal gives unobstructed access to the wig, and the tray base depth can be specified to the exact hair length of the product range. Standard wig boxes range from 30 cm to 50 cm in length depending on the product. Magnetic-flap boxes suit shorter or coiled presentation wigs where the flip-open motion adds a gifting register.

"A 40 cm lift-off wig box in 2.0 mm greyboard with matte-black laminate and gold hot-foil logo can be produced at Huamei from 200 pieces with samples in 7–10 days."

Drawer-format boxes are less common for wigs because the pull motion compresses items horizontally — workable for wigs with volume control, less suitable for delicate textures. See [rigid box manufacturing](/craft/rigid) for a comparison of lid formats and their structural constraints.

## What branding and surface decoration options suit wig packaging?

Hot-foil stamping on the box lid is the most legible logo application method for dark-ground wig packaging. Gold and silver foil against black, navy, or deep green laminate delivers high contrast without additional print cost. Huamei holds [seventeen curated hot-foil colours in-house](/craft/hot-foil), which eliminates the minimum-order constraints of specialty foil sourcing.

Spot-UV over matte lamination creates a gloss-against-matte contrast for logo panels — useful for brands that want a tactile premium feel without emboss tooling cost. Emboss or deboss logos work on lighter ground colours where hot-foil contrast would be lower. Screen printing is an option for multi-colour logos on smaller volume runs where offset litho setup cost cannot be spread across the quantity.

"Over 80% of Huamei's factory energy comes from solar generation — a meaningful ESG signal for wig brands sourcing under BSCI or FSC audit conditions."

## What insert and lining options protect wigs in transit?

Wig boxes need an interior that prevents the wig from shifting and compressing against box walls. Satin lining glued to a card insert is the standard approach — smooth against the wig fibres and adds a tactile quality reference to the opening experience. Paper-pulp moulded inserts are suitable for brands targeting [FSC](https://fsc.org/en) chain-of-custody compliance, as they use certified material throughout. The [Man Made Crayon case study](/volumes/man-made-crayon) shows a rigid book-style format with a fabric-lined insert, which uses the same construction principle scaled to a different product type.

EVA foam cut to the wig crown profile is the highest-protection option for long hair or curled styles, preventing compression during stacked transit. Foam inserts add slightly more weight per box, which can affect freight cost calculations under [ICC Incoterms 2020](https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/) FOB terms.

## What MOQ, lead times, and certifications should wig brands plan for?

Huamei's public MOQ floor is 200 pieces, with samples in 7–10 days and production runs in 15–20 days. Wig packaging briefings typically require a sample round to confirm insert fit — the box geometry must be matched to the specific wig format, so one to two sample iterations are standard before production approval.

"Huamei holds [amfori BSCI](https://www.amfori.org/en/tools-and-services/amfori-bsci), CE, EQS, FSC, and SGS certifications, satisfying the supplier-approval requirements of most EU and US beauty retail chains that run social and environmental audits."

Transit testing at Huamei includes high 50 °C and low −30 °C environmental simulation and 24-hour vibration, drop, and compression tests — the full export-grade protocol. For wig packaging shipped by ocean container to US or EU, these tests confirm that lid fitment and insert geometry hold through temperature swings and freight handling.
