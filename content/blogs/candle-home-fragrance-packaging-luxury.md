# Luxury candle and home fragrance packaging: structures, finishes, and specifications

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 18 May 2026. Updated 18 May 2026.

Sonia Sun has produced packaging for wellness, botanical, and fragrance brands at Huamei since founding the company in Zhengzhou in 1992 — including work for [Glees Grove Soaps](/volumes/glees-grove), a natural-register wellness brand whose packaging uses an uncoated botanical paper with no metallic foil, and [Wild Black Berry by Heigouqi](/volumes/heigouqi), a sleeve-and-tray format with botanical illustration.

Candle packaging has a structural brief that differs from most other luxury categories in one specific respect: the primary packaging is glass, and glass vessels require rigid box inserts that hold the vessel under compression without contacting the rim. A box that meets the aesthetic requirements of a premium candle brand must simultaneously pass transit-grade testing — 24-hour vibration simulation, drop testing, and temperature cycling from +50 °C to -30 °C — without allowing the glass to shift or the outer box to deform. Starting the brief from the vessel dimensions and weight, not from the outer box aesthetic, produces better outcomes.

## What packaging structures work for luxury candle products?

> Luxury candle packaging most commonly uses two rigid box structures: the two-piece nested box (an outer lid over a base with a custom die-cut paper insert that holds the vessel) and the magnetic-flap box with a cavity insert that suspends the vessel at the correct height. Both structures are available from MOQ 200 pieces with 7–10 day samples and 15–20 day production runs.

The two-piece nested box — a lid that slides over a base — is the more common format at low volumes because it requires no magnet tooling and is straightforward to hand-assemble. The lid sits over the base at 3–5 mm overlap, which locks the box during transit without a separate closure mechanism. The base carries a hand-assembled paper insert that cradles the vessel; the insert is die-cut to the vessel's outer diameter and folded to form a collar that holds the glass at the correct height with 1–2 mm clearance above the insert rim.

The magnetic-flap [rigid box](/craft/rigid) is specified when the opening experience is a priority — for gifting and retail contexts where the box will be handled by the recipient before the candle is used. The lid lifts on two neodymium closures calibrated to 6–50 grams of pull-force at 2,800 Gauss, and the vessel is presented in a custom cavity insert. The cavity insert is usually paper-covered foam board or folded card stock, depending on the vessel weight and the retail price tier.

## What board weight protects a glass candle vessel in transit?

A luxury candle box requires 2.0–2.5 mm greyboard for the outer shell to provide the structural compression resistance that a glass vessel demands.

The outer shell must survive Huamei's transit-grade testing protocol before production sign-off: high-temperature exposure at 50 °C (simulating container transit in direct sun or hot-climate storage), low-temperature exposure at -30 °C (cold-chain distribution or northern winter delivery), 24-hour vibration simulation, drop testing from four orientations, and empty-box compression testing under stacking load. At 2.0 mm greyboard with a PVA-bonded wrap paper, the outer shell maintains its geometry through all five test modes without deforming the insert cavity.

The insert is typically lighter — 1.5 mm board or paper-covered foam — because its job is to absorb vessel movement, not to carry structural load. For vessels above 400 g (a 300 ml candle in a heavy glass finish), the insert uses a dual-wall construction: an inner collar die-cut to the vessel profile and an outer perimeter base that distributes the vessel weight across the full insert footprint.

## What surface finishes communicate the home fragrance register?

The home fragrance register in luxury packaging favours matte and natural surfaces over gloss and metallic — a quieter finish set than spirits or cosmetic categories, aligned with the interiors and lifestyle context in which candles and fragrance products are typically used.

Matte or [soft-touch lamination](/craft/soft-touch) on an uncoated or lightly coated base board is the dominant surface choice. Soft-touch lamination creates a dry, velvet-like surface that communicates tactile quality before the box is opened; matte uncoated board retains a natural fibre texture and takes emboss and deboss with clean, sharp definition. [Glees Grove Soaps](/volumes/glees-grove) uses an uncoated botanical paper with no laminate — the surface communicates natural materials by feel, which aligns with the brand's ingredient sourcing positioning.

[FSC](https://fsc.org/en) chain-of-custody certification is available for all Huamei candle packaging orders. FSC certification traces the board and wrap paper fibre from a sustainably managed forest through each production step to the finished box — a requirement for UK, EU, and North American fragrance brands with responsible-sourcing commitments. Huamei holds FSC as part of its five active international certifications (BSCI, CE, EQS, FSC, SGS). Over 80% of Huamei's factory energy comes from solar generation, which supports a fragrance brand's supply-chain sustainability narrative.

Hot-foil stamping is used sparingly on candle and fragrance packaging — typically a single colour applied to the brand mark or a small botanical motif. The [Wild Black Berry by Heigouqi](/volumes/heigouqi) wellness sleeve uses no foil; decoration is entirely print-based, with botanical illustration on an uncoated sleeve in a register that reads provenance rather than ceremony.

## How do MOQ and lead times work for custom candle packaging?

Custom candle packaging from Huamei runs at MOQ 200+ pieces with 7–10 day samples and 15–20 day production runs from sample sign-off.

The insert is the variable that most commonly extends the sample timeline. If the insert is a standard die-cut collar — the most common configuration — it is included within the 7–10 day sample window. If the insert requires a custom-tooled foam profile or a moulded pulp tray for vessels with irregular profiles, add 2–3 days for insert tooling fabrication.

Repeat orders for existing structures run faster: the outer box die and wrap-paper print plates are on file, and the insert die is stored at the factory. A second-season candle packaging order for a known structure can confirm in 5–8 days' sample review and 15 days' production at volumes above 500 pieces.
