# Mid-Autumn Festival 2026 packaging production calendar: brief now, ship by September

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 11 June 2026. Updated 11 June 2026.

Sonia Sun has overseen seasonal packaging production at Huamei since 1992 — from the first Mid-Autumn run to the current programme, including mooncake gift sets, spirits festival SKUs, and premium confectionery formats that require a precisely managed brief-to-ship calendar to arrive before the festival date.

Mid-Autumn Festival 2026 falls on 17 September. That date is fourteen weeks from today. Rigid box packaging with hot-foil decoration requires 7–10 days for sampling and 15–20 days for production — a minimum of five weeks from artwork lock to packaging in hand, before freight is added. This is not a tight window when a brief starts now. It becomes a missed window when a brief starts in August. This page is the 2026 calendar — the specific dates for each packaging type, the structure choices that fit mooncake and gifting formats, and the decoration constraints that matter for a September deadline.

## When should brands brief Mid-Autumn 2026 gift packaging?

Brands targeting Mid-Autumn Festival on 17 September 2026 should brief their packaging in June 2026. The critical calendar: brief and artwork lock by 28 July, sample approval by 8 August, production run 8–28 August, in-hands by 10 September. Briefing after 28 July risks missing the in-hands window or compressing to a single sample revision.

The 28 July artwork-lock date is the last viable start point, not the recommended one. A brief submitted now — in June — allows two rounds of sample revision, the option to test two structure variants, and a comfortable domestic freight window after production completes. A brief submitted on July 28 compresses to one revision round and one structure option. The difference between June and late July is not weeks of lead time; it is the ability to correct a sample that does not match the brief.

"Mid-Autumn 2026 falls on 17 September — a brief submitted in June allows two sample revisions; a brief submitted after 28 July compresses to one revision and eliminates the buffer for a structural correction."

## What packaging structures work best for Mid-Autumn gifting?

Three rigid box structures are most commonly specified for Mid-Autumn gifting: the two-piece telescoping lid box for mooncake sets, the magnetic rigid box for premium cross-category gifting, and the drawer-and-slipcase for single-SKU presentation.

The [two-piece telescoping rigid box](/craft/rigid) is the standard mooncake format: a shallow tray base with a deep lid that lifts off to reveal the arrangement. Standard dimensions for a 4-piece set run approximately 200 × 200 × 55 mm; an 8-piece set runs approximately 300 × 200 × 55 mm. The lid is the primary branding surface — hot-foil on a deep colour ground in the festival palette of deep red, midnight navy, or burnt gold.

The [magnetic rigid box](/craft/magnetic) suits premium non-food gifting: a double-layer or clamshell format for tea canisters, cosmetic sets, or spirits miniatures, with a magnetic front flap rather than a lift-off lid. Pull-force on Huamei's magnetic closures ranges from 6 to 50 g at 2,800 Gauss — a pull-force of 20–35 g is typical for a premium festival gift, firm enough to hold securely in packaging, light enough for a recipient to open without effort.

"A magnetic rigid box for Mid-Autumn gifting should specify pull-force between 20 and 35 g at 2,800 Gauss — firm enough to hold the lid securely during gift wrapping, light enough for recipients to open without difficulty."

## What decoration choices define Mid-Autumn packaging?

The Mid-Autumn visual register is anchored to three elements: warm metallic foil in gold, bronze, or deep rose-gold; a saturated dark colour ground (deep red, navy, forest green); and harvest-referenced motifs — the full moon, osmanthus blossom, palace silhouette, or the jade rabbit. These elements are consistent across the category and expected by gift recipients; the design challenge is distinguishing within the register, not departing from it.

Huamei holds [seventeen curated hot-foil colours in-house](/craft/hot-foil), with the gold palette covering champagne, warm gold, deep antique gold, and rich bronze. Hot-foil is pressed at 120–160 °C with registration held to ±0.1 mm against any emboss element. A registered emboss — foil and sculpted die landing on the same surface — is the correct specification for a festival hero box where the brand mark needs to carry full visual weight.

The [Yangshao case study](/volumes/yangshao) demonstrates registered foil-and-deboss coordination on a premium spirits format with the same warm metallic register as Mid-Autumn gifting — the reference for programs where a single metallic element on a deep ground is the entire design language.

Two wrap surface options for Mid-Autumn programmes: a deep matte laminate on coated art paper (standard, offset-printable, high surface precision) and a book-cloth or textured uncoated sheet (premium, quieter, more archival register). Both are within the standard sampling window. [FSC](https://fsc.org/en)-certified grades are available in both.

- Production sign-off: 8 August

- Production sign-off: 2 August
- Production run: 3 – 22 August

- Brief and artwork lock: 7 July 2026

"A complex Mid-Autumn 2026 programme with two sample rounds and registered emboss-and-foil must be briefed no later than 7 July — three days past that date and the second sample round cannot complete before the production window opens."

Brands with FSC supply-chain documentation requirements should confirm FSC material selection at the brief stage, not at the production stage.
