# Magnetic gift box packaging: structure, pull-force, and how to specify it

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 30 May 2026. Updated 30 May 2026.

Sonia Sun has designed and built magnetic closure packaging across four factories in Henan, Zhejiang, Sichuan, and Guizhou since founding Huamei in Zhengzhou in 1992 — for gifting, cosmetic, tea, and wellness brands from first-run DTC to large-volume branded series. Notes on rigid box construction and closure mechanics are at [magnetic closure boxes](/craft/magnetic) and [rigid box structures](/craft/rigid).

The magnetic closure is the moment a gift box reveals itself. The lid lifts away without a gap to pry, the magnets engage at a tactile click, and the box closes to a finish that reads as complete rather than merely shut. That sequence — open, experience, close — is the functional brief for magnetic gift box packaging, and the engineering behind it is more specific than it looks. This guide covers the construction, the pull-force range, the surface options, and what a complete brief includes.

## What is magnetic gift box packaging?

> Magnetic gift box packaging is a rigid box construction where neodymium magnets are embedded in the greyboard lid and base wall, closing the box with a controlled snap. Pull-force runs 6–50 g at 2,800 Gauss depending on closure type and board weight. MOQ starts at 200 pieces.

Magnetic gift boxes belong to the rigid box family. The core structure is greyboard — typically 1.5–2.5 mm thick for the lid and base walls — wrapped in printed paper or cloth and assembled by hand. What distinguishes a magnetic gift box from a standard lift-off lid is the pair of neodymium magnets sealed inside the board sandwich: one in the lid, one in the corresponding position on the base wall. When the lid closes, the magnet pair aligns and draws the lid flush.

Huamei holds magnetic closure pull-force to 6–50 g at 2,800 Gauss across closure types and board weights. The lower end of that range — 6–15 g — produces a soft, guided close: the lid is drawn toward shut but requires almost no finger pressure to seat fully. The upper end — 35–50 g — produces a definite snap and a box that holds its position even when inverted. The right pull-force depends on the product weight, the box orientation during display or transit, and the opening ritual the brand intends.

## How are magnets embedded in a rigid gift box?

Magnets are recessed into the greyboard before wrapping, so the finished exterior shows no trace of the closure mechanism.

The process: a recess is die-cut into the greyboard wall at the closure point. A neodymium disc or bar magnet is seated in the recess and fixed with adhesive. A second layer of greyboard or liner paper is laminated over the top, sealing the magnet flush with the board surface. The wrap paper then goes over the assembled board sandwich in the normal way, so the finished box exterior is smooth paper — no hardware visible.

The magnet position must be consistent across every unit in a production run. Huamei's fixture tooling holds magnet placement to a positional tolerance that keeps the lid alignment repeatable through transit, handling, and repeated opening cycles.

## What surface decorations work with magnetic gift boxes?

Every finish available for standard rigid boxes applies to magnetic gift box packaging — hot foil, emboss, deboss, spot UV, soft-touch laminate, and offset print — because the magnetic closure is contained inside the board structure, not on the surface.

The one design consideration specific to magnetic boxes is that foil and emboss work near the closure point require accurate registration to the board panel, not to the magnet position. On a lid that overhangs the base by 1–2 mm, a foil motif crossing the closure edge must be designed with that overhang in mind. The [hot-foil stamping process](/craft/hot-foil) holds registration to ±0.1 mm across the face panel.

Soft-touch laminate is a common first choice for magnetic gift boxes in cosmetic and gifting applications: the matte, velvet surface signals luxury before the box is opened and does not accumulate fingerprints at the closure point. [Collgene's skincare packaging](/volumes/collgene) uses a soft-touch surface with a restrained foil mark — a combination well suited to the cosmetic register.

For a tactile alternative without laminate, an uncoated textured paper wrap introduces grain that reads under the fingertip. [Man Made Crayon](/volumes/man-made-crayon) demonstrates what a kraft wrap achieves in a gifting context: a material signal distinct from the coated luxury register, appropriate for artisan and DTC positioning.

## What greyboard weight should a magnetic gift box use?

Greyboard between 1.5 mm and 2.5 mm covers the majority of magnetic gift box applications, with 2.0 mm as the standard for cosmetic and gifting.

Thinner board — 1.5 mm — suits smaller formats where total box weight and shipping cube are constraints. The lid snap is adequate at this weight with a 2,800-Gauss magnet pair in the 15–25 g pull-force range. Thicker board — 2.5 mm — is appropriate for heavier products, larger formats, or premium single-SKU presentation boxes where the board weight itself signals substance.

The greyboard weight also affects the register of the wrapped edge at the closure line. A 2.0 mm board wraps a tighter, more precise lid edge than a 1.5 mm board on the same structure geometry. For a box where the lid edge is a visible design element, specifying 2.0 mm is the correct default.

## What does a complete magnetic gift box brief include?

A brief that moves to sample without a back-and-forth round specifies: box format (lid-and-base, clamshell, or drawer-with-lid); finished dimensions (external L × W × D in mm for lid and base separately); greyboard weight for lid walls and base; pull-force target in grams; wrap paper type, weight, and surface (coated art / textured / specialty sheet); primary surface decoration (foil colour, emboss depth, laminate type); quantity; and destination port for shipping.

Huamei holds seventeen curated foil colours in-house and eighty papers on file for wrap application. For most magnetic gift box briefs, structure and surface can be confirmed from stock without an extended sourcing step. Sample cycle is 7–10 days from artwork and specification lock; production follows at 15–20 days. MOQ floor is 200+ pieces.

[FSC](https://fsc.org/en) chain-of-custody certification covers paper and board sourcing for brands with sustainable procurement requirements. Huamei holds BSCI, CE, EQS, FSC, and SGS certifications — documentation available for procurement audit packages.
