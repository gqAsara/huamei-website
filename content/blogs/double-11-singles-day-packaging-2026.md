# Double 11 Singles' Day packaging 2026: production calendar and structural specifications

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 24 May 2026. Updated 24 May 2026.

Double 11 — November 11, 2026 — is the largest single-day e-commerce event by volume globally, with cosmetics, spirits, tea, and wellness categories among the highest-GMV segments on Tmall and JD. For brands that sell in mainland China or ship to Chinese-diaspora markets, the packaging decision for a Double 11 SKU must be locked by late June to have any reasonable buffer before the go-live date. This article covers the production calendar, structural requirements, and briefing specifics that separate a package that arrives on time from one that misses the window. For the gifting framing, see [luxury packaging for e-commerce transit](/blogs/luxury-packaging-ecommerce-transit-protection).

Sonia Sun has run Huamei's press floors since founding the company in Zhengzhou in 1992 — over three decades of seasonal packaging production for spirits, cosmetics, and gifting brands, across four factories in Henan, Zhejiang, Sichuan, and Guizhou.

## When should I brief my packaging manufacturer for Double 11 Singles' Day 2026?

> For Double 11 on 11 November 2026, submit the packaging brief no later than late June. Samples take 7–10 days; production runs 15–20 days. Add 1–3 days domestic trucking in mainland China, or 4–6 weeks ocean freight for international buyers. Total cycle from locked brief to warehouse is 8–10 weeks.

The calendar math is tighter than most brands expect. Working back from a November 1 warehouse-receipt target (10 days before go-live, allowing for last-mile staging):

| Milestone | Target date |
|---|---|
| Brief submitted and confirmed | 30 June 2026 |
| Artwork locked and sampling begins | 7 July 2026 |
| Sample approval | 18–21 July 2026 |
| Production run begins | 22 July 2026 |
| Production complete + QC | 12–15 August 2026 |
| Domestic freight departure | 16 August 2026 |
| Warehouse receipt (domestic China) | 19–20 August 2026 |

Brands briefing after 30 June can still hit November 11, but the buffer disappears. Any artwork revision that adds a sample loop costs 7–10 days; a late brief with no revision headroom is the most common cause of a missed launch date.

## What structural specifications does Double 11 packaging require?

Double 11 packaging must pass e-commerce transit standards — each unit ships individually through Cainiao, SF Express, or JD Logistics, without the protection of a master carton. The [ISTA](https://www.ista.org/) 3A protocol covers single-parcel e-commerce shipments; Huamei's transit-grade testing aligns with this protocol, including drop, vibration, and compression under the weight of stacked parcels in a sortation facility.

For a luxury [rigid box](/craft/rigid), the structural specifications that matter most for Double 11 are: The construction context for this sits in [custom luxury rigid box manufacturing](/blogs/custom-luxury-rigid-box-manufacturing), which walks through greyboard, wrapping, and assembly tolerances end-to-end.

- **Greyboard weight:** minimum 1,500 gsm for boxes above 200 mm in any dimension. Lighter board compresses under sortation stacking.
- **Closure specification:** a [magnetic closure](/craft/magnetic) specified at 6–50 g pull-force at 2,800 Gauss holds through the handling impacts of sortation and last-mile delivery. A weaker closure opens in transit and arrives with the box ajar.
- **Outer protection:** most brands shipping luxury rigid boxes through Chinese e-commerce logistics specify a corrugated outer carton per unit, not per pallet. The outer carton is separate from the presentation rigid box; its dimensions must be drawn into the structural brief at the same time as the rigid box.
- **Insert specification:** product inserts must hold the product through the ISTA 3A drop sequence. An insert that passes the factory QC table but has not been specified to ISTA standards will allow product movement.

## Which product categories drive Double 11 packaging volume?

Cosmetics and skincare are the largest packaging category on Double 11 by unit volume. Spirits and baijiu drive the highest average-unit packaging value — the gift-grade packaging for a Double 11 spirits SKU is often the most elaborately decorated piece in the brand's annual run. Tea, wellness, and supplement categories have grown significantly since 2023, and their packaging briefs tend toward sleeve-and-tray or book-style rigid constructions with foil and soft-touch lamination.

[Collgene](/volumes/collgene), a skincare client, is an example of a cosmetic brand whose Double 11 SKU packaging runs on the same structural specification as its year-round range, with a limited-edition outer wrap and foil variant produced in a smaller parallel run. [Wuliangye 68](/volumes/wuliangye-68), a premium spirits case, represents the opposite approach: the Double 11 SKU is a structurally distinct collector edition, requiring a separate structural brief and die set.

## What surface decoration suits Double 11 packaging?

The most requested surface decoration combination for a Double 11 limited edition is: a soft-touch lamination outer, a gold or rose-gold foil brand mark, and a registered emboss at the foil position. This combination reads as premium at the point of unboxing and holds up through the e-commerce transit conditions described above.

Hot-foil stamping registration at Huamei is held to ±0.1 mm. A registered emboss — foil over a raised surface — requires this tolerance to land correctly. A Double 11 brief that specifies registered emboss but does not lock the registration tolerance will receive a sample that may show drift, requiring a tooling correction that costs 3–5 days.

Huamei's seventeen on-file foil colours are available for Double 11 runs. Limited-edition colourways outside the on-file palette require a custom foil procurement step, adding 5–7 days to the pre-production phase.

## ESG and the Double 11 brief

Double 11's scale makes it a visible moment for brand-level ESG commitment. Buyers qualifying Chinese packaging suppliers increasingly require [FSC](https://fsc.org/en)-certified paper on any packaging they will market as "sustainable" in their Double 11 campaign copy. Huamei holds FSC certification; the on-file paper palette includes FSC-certified stocks in the weight and texture ranges used for Double 11 rigid-box construction.

Huamei's factories run on more than 80% green energy, primarily solar, across all four sites. For brands whose Double 11 campaign includes a sustainability message, the factory energy profile is a reportable supply-chain fact — not an aspirational claim.
