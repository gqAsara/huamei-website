# Black Friday gift packaging 2026: briefing calendar and structure guide for North American brands

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 21 July 2026. Updated 21 July 2026.

Sonia Sun has produced seasonal and promotional gift packaging at Huamei's four factories in Henan, Zhejiang, Sichuan, and Guizhou since founding the company in Zhengzhou in 1992, supplying North American brands under FOB terms for Q4 delivery windows including Black Friday and the broader holiday season.

Black Friday 2026 falls on November 28. For North American brands sourcing custom gift packaging from China, that date is already close: the production calendar, ocean freight schedule, and US customs clearance together consume six to eight weeks from the moment an approved brief reaches the factory. July is when the sourcing decision needs to be made, not October.

The [National Retail Federation](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends) consistently reports Black Friday as one of the highest-volume gifting weekends of the year in the US market. The packaging brief should reflect that context: the box is not a standalone product, it is the first physical signal a recipient receives about the gift inside.

## When should North American brands brief a China factory for Black Friday gift packaging?

> North American brands should brief a China packaging factory for Black Friday 2026 no later than mid-September. With a 15–20 day production run and 12–16 days of ocean freight under FOB terms, any brief arriving after October 1 risks missing the November 28 delivery window.

The production calendar works backward from delivery. US ports need the container 12–16 transit days ahead of the need date, depending on the port of origin and the routing. Before the container can move, the production run must complete — 15–20 days from artwork approval. Before production, sampling runs 7–10 days from the approved brief. That is six to eight weeks minimum from brief to port.

A brief arriving at the factory in mid-September allows:
- 7–10 days for sampling and client approval
- 15–20 days for the production run
- 2–3 days for quality inspection, packing, and inland freight to port
- 14–16 days ocean freight FOB Qingdao or FOB Shanghai to US West Coast ports

The buffer shrinks fast. A round of sample revisions costs another 5–7 days. A brief arriving in October with anything more than minor amendments risks missing the window entirely.

## What gift box structures work best for Black Friday 2026 packaging?

Two formats account for the majority of Black Friday gift packaging produced at Huamei.

**Magnetic closure rigid box.** The magnetic closure box presents well in a retail or DTC unboxing context: the lid lifts with a single motion, the magnetic close seats with a definite snap, and the box holds its shape through the supply chain without a secondary shipper for most product weights. Magnetic pull-force at Huamei runs 6–50 grams at 2,800 Gauss across closure types and board weights — specifying the right closure for the product weight is part of the brief. The full guide to magnetic closure boxes is at [/craft/magnetic](/craft/magnetic).

**Two-piece lift-off rigid box.** The lift-off lid format is the simpler alternative — lower tooling cost, faster sample cycle, and the same greyboard and wrap-paper range as any other rigid format. The lid removes cleanly, reveals the interior, and requires no magnetic hardware. For gift sets with a strong unboxing visual (tissue-lined interiors, ribbon pull tabs), the two-piece format is often the right call.

Ninety-nine structures are on file at Huamei; the [rigid box structure library](/craft/rigid) covers the full range from drawer formats to telescope configurations.

## What surface finishes are most ordered for Black Friday gift packaging?

Gold and warm metallic foil, soft-touch laminate, and spot-UV are the three finishes most specified for Black Friday gift packaging — the same palette used across the broader Q4 gifting season.

**Hot-foil gold and warm metallics.** Seventeen curated foil colours are available in-house, covering the full gold-to-bronze-to-rose-gold spectrum that dominates Q4 gift packaging. Hot-foil at ±0.1 mm registration allows fine brand wordmarks and graphic details to stamp cleanly on the wrap paper.

**Soft-touch laminate.** The velvet-matte surface photographs without glare and holds colour accurately under retail lighting. It is the standard outer finish for high-end gifting and contrasts well against foil-stamped areas on the same sheet.

**Spot-UV.** A high-gloss selective coating on a matte base draws attention to specific areas of the design — typically a brand icon or a geometric accent pattern. Registration tolerance for spot-UV is ±0.2 mm, which is sufficient for most Black Friday gifting applications.

## What are the MOQ and certifications for Black Friday gift packaging?

MOQ for custom Black Friday gift boxes starts at 200+ pieces. Sample lead time is 7–10 days from approved artwork; production lead time is 15–20 days.

Huamei holds BSCI, CE, EQS, [FSC](https://fsc.org/en), and SGS certifications — relevant for US corporate procurement teams that require supplier social and environmental compliance documentation. The factories operate on more than 80% green energy, primarily solar, across all four manufacturing sites.

For North American brands shipping under FOB terms, cost and risk transfer at the named port of loading — typically Qingdao or Shanghai. Freight, insurance, and US customs brokerage are the buyer's responsibility from that point. The [ICC Incoterms 2020 framework](https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/) defines each term's obligations precisely.

## How does Black Friday gift packaging differ from Christmas gift packaging?

The structural brief is identical. The aesthetic register shifts.

Christmas gift packaging draws from a well-established colour vocabulary: red, green, gold, white, kraft. Black Friday gift packaging runs earlier in the Q4 season and targets a slightly different register — the gift is promotional as much as celebratory. Dark base colours (navy, charcoal, deep green) with warm gold foil are the most common brief direction. The brand identity usually takes more prominence than seasonal decoration.

The briefing calendar is nearly identical: both windows require an early-October production start to hit US delivery. A brand producing packaging for both events can run them as two concurrent briefs, specify the same structure and tooling for both, and differentiate only in wrap paper, foil, and any seasonal print elements. This reduces tooling cost and simplifies the sampling cycle.

Brands that have run seasonal gift packaging programmes with Huamei include [Glees Grove](/volumes/glees-grove) and [Man Made Crayon](/volumes/man-made-crayon) — both illustrate how consistent structure choice across seasonal drops reduces lead time and per-unit cost.

## Starting a Black Friday packaging brief

A complete brief for Black Friday packaging includes: finished box dimensions (L × W × H in mm), board weight (1.5 mm, 2.0 mm, or 2.5 mm), structure type (magnetic closure, two-piece, or other), wrap paper direction or colour, foil locations and colour, quantity, and FOB port preference.

The earlier the brief arrives, the more revision cycles are available within the production window. For Black Friday 2026, mid-September is the deadline. For DTC brands that want sample photography for pre-launch assets, late August is a better target — it allows the sample to arrive, be photographed, and the production brief to confirm without compromising the ship date.
