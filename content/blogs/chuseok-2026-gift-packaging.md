# Chuseok 2026 gift packaging: rigid box planning calendar for the Korean premium gifting season

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 11 August 2026. Updated 11 August 2026.

Sonia Sun has produced gift packaging for Korean premium brands and distributors at Huamei since the early 2000s, managing seasonal programmes that ship from Henan and Zhejiang factories to Korean retail and gifting channels — where Chuseok represents the single largest premium gifting event of the calendar year.

Chuseok (추석) is the Korean autumn harvest festival, falling on the 15th day of the 8th lunar month each year. For 2026, that date is **17 September**. The [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/) describes Chuseok as one of the most significant holidays in the Korean calendar — a three-day period centred on family gatherings, ancestral memorial rites, and the exchange of premium gift sets. In terms of packaging volume, it is comparable to the Chinese Mid-Autumn Festival that falls on the same lunar date. For brands and distributors sourcing [rigid gift boxes](/craft/rigid) from China, Chuseok requires the same advance planning as any major Chinese festival — and the calendar is already short.

## When is Chuseok 2026 and what is the briefing deadline for custom packaging?

Chuseok 2026 falls on 17 September. Brands sourcing rigid gift boxes from a Chinese manufacturer with a 15–20 day production lead time and 7–10 day sampling window should confirm a signed-off design brief by 10–15 August to receive finished goods before the holiday begins.

The production arithmetic is precise. From a signed-off design brief to first physical sample: 7–10 days. From sample approval to production-run completion: 15–20 days. Add 5–7 days for international freight to Korean ports. The total minimum window is 27–37 days from brief to delivery — which places the hard deadline for a Chuseok 2026 order at approximately 11–21 August for a 17 September arrival.

"Chuseok 2026 falls on 17 September — brands sourcing rigid gift boxes from China need a confirmed design brief by 15 August to meet the 15–20 day production lead time and allow time for freight."

## What packaging structures work for Chuseok gift sets?

Chuseok gift sets centre on premium food items — Korean pears, beef, dried seafood, and confectionery — alongside cosmetics, wellness products, and premium spirits. Each category has a distinct structural logic.

For food gift sets, the dominant structure is the [rigid lift-off or clamshell box](/craft/rigid) with an internal tray or division insert that holds items upright and separated. A standard Chuseok food gift set in the 400–600g range typically uses a two-piece lift-off rigid box with a 1.5–2.0 mm greyboard core, a 128–170 gsm wrapping paper, and a shaped EVA or moulded-pulp insert. The insert specification matters: Chuseok gift sets reach retailers three to five weeks before the festival and must withstand transit and display handling before the recipient opens them.

For cosmetics and wellness, drawer boxes and magnetic closure boxes are the dominant formats, consistent with year-round K-beauty packaging conventions. The Chuseok season shifts the colour register and surface treatment rather than the structure type.

## How should Chuseok packaging differ from Chinese New Year design?

Chuseok packaging uses a warmer, harvest-toned palette than Chinese New Year: golds, deep greens, and earthy burgundy rather than the bright vermilion and pure gold of CNY.

The distinction matters because the same Chinese manufacturer supplies both markets, and a Chuseok programme should not look like a CNY programme with a different label. The Chuseok design register draws on harvest imagery — moon motifs, autumn leaves, harvest grains, traditional Korean latticework (창살, changssal) — executed in deep ochre, pine green, and burnished gold. The [hot-foil](/craft/hot-foil) colour selection shifts accordingly: antique gold and bronze foils are specified more often than the bright mirror gold common in CNY packaging.

Huamei holds seventeen curated foil colours in-house, including warm gold, antique gold, champagne, copper, and bronze tones that suit the Chuseok palette. A sample of candidate foils on the selected wrapping paper is the fastest way to confirm which gold reads correctly against the chosen ground colour.

"Chuseok gift packaging uses a harvest-toned palette — deep ochre, pine green, and burnished gold — rather than the bright vermilion and mirror gold of CNY. The foil selection shifts to antique gold and bronze to match."

## What is the production calendar for Chuseok 2026 packaging from China?

Brands sourcing from China for Chuseok 2026 must complete production before 8 September to allow time for international freight — the total window from brief to Korea delivery is 27–37 days, leaving no room for revision rounds after 15 August.

| Action | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Design brief confirmed, paper and foil references sent | 11–15 August 2026 |
| Sample approval (receive physical sample, confirm) | 22–25 August 2026 |
| Production run confirmed, PO released | 25 August 2026 |
| Production complete, goods ready for freight | 8–14 September 2026 |
| Goods arrive at Korean port (sea freight from Qingdao/Shanghai) | 13–17 September 2026 |

Air freight from Zhengzhou or Shanghai adds cost but compresses the freight leg to 2–3 days, creating a buffer for late sample revisions. MOQ for Chuseok programmes is 200+ pieces, consistent with Huamei's public floor across all structured gift box formats.

For brands running a Chuseok programme alongside a Chinese Mid-Autumn Festival programme, both holidays fall on 17 September 2026 — the same production run can supply both markets if the design is managed to work across both cultural contexts, or parallel runs can be produced from the same tooling with different wrapping papers.

## What certifications do Korean buyers require from Chinese packaging manufacturers?

Korean buyers increasingly require FSC chain-of-custody documentation and BSCI or equivalent social-responsibility audits from Chinese packaging suppliers.

[FSC certification](https://fsc.org/en) confirms that the paper and board used in the packaging comes from responsibly managed forests. The Korean Act on the Promotion of Saving and Recycling of Resources drives FSC specification at major Korean retail accounts; buyers sourcing for Lotte, Shinsegae, and Hyundai Department Store gift programmes routinely request FSC documentation in their supplier audits. Huamei holds FSC chain-of-custody certification, and the documentation package includes the FSC certificate and transaction verification for inclusion in the buyer's compliance file.

BSCI (Business Social Compliance Initiative) is the most commonly required social-responsibility credential for Korean corporate buyers and major retail accounts. Huamei holds BSCI certification on file. Additional certifications on file include CE, EQS, and SGS.

"FSC chain-of-custody certification and BSCI audit credentials are the two most commonly required supplier documents in Korean retail packaging procurement. Huamei holds both on file."
