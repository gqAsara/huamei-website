By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 2026-07-25. Updated 2026-07-25.

Sonia Sun has produced skincare and personal care packaging on Huamei's Henan press floor since founding the company in 1992 — from single-SKU cartons to multi-bottle gift sets for retail and e-commerce gifting.

Hair care has become one of the most packaging-conscious categories in personal care. Brands that previously shipped products in generic cartons are investing in rigid box gift sets — for seasonal retail, for direct-to-consumer subscription launches, and for the travel-sized treatment collections that have become a standard product configuration. This guide covers what matters structurally and from a finish perspective when commissioning a rigid box for a hair care set.

## What rigid box structure works best for a hair care set?

A magnetic-closure lid-and-base rigid box with individual EVA foam cavities for each bottle is the standard for premium hair care gift sets. The insert prevents bottles from touching during transit, and the magnetic closure delivers the tactile quality signal the category requires.

The structure choice depends on the number of bottles and their configuration. The most common hair care set configurations and their structural implications:

**Two-bottle set (shampoo + conditioner, or shampoo + treatment).** A lid-and-base rigid box with two side-by-side cavities in a moulded insert. This is the most straightforward configuration. The internal dimension needs to account for bottle-cap height — most shampoo caps are 30–50 mm taller than the bottle body — to ensure the lid closes flush without the bottles being compressed against it.

**Three- to four-bottle collection (full treatment range).** A wider lid-and-base or a book-style clamshell. At three bottles across, the external width starts to exceed standard gift box proportions; a landscape orientation (wider than tall) is more common for multi-bottle collections than the portrait format used for cosmetics. [Insert design](/craft/inserts) for a four-bottle set should separate each bottle individually — not use a shared cavity — to prevent label-to-label contact and foil marking during transit.

**Shampoo bar and treatment oil set.** Where one item is solid and one is liquid, the insert strategy changes: the solid product doesn't need a sealed cavity, but the liquid oil bottle does. A hybrid insert — formed EVA for the oil, a tray recess for the solid — is the practical approach at Huamei.

## How surface finish choices apply to hair care gift packaging

Hair care gift packaging has converged on two finish registers: the natural/artisanal look (uncoated kraft or textured paper, matte lamination, minimal foil) and the premium-performance look (coated art paper, soft-touch lamination, silver or gold foil). Both read differently on shelf and to the end recipient.

[Soft-touch lamination](/craft/soft-touch) is the most requested surface finish for premium hair care gift boxes. It gives the exterior a slightly matte, velvet-feel surface that reads as premium without being formal. The operational note: soft-touch on a dark or saturated paper base shows fingerprints less than soft-touch on a light paper. For a white or cream gift box — common in the scalp-treatment and professional-grade segment — this is worth specifying: a fingerprint-resistant soft-touch grade is available and should be named on the brief.

"Huamei applies soft-touch lamination across both coated and uncoated paper substrates — on a dark base, it minimises fingerprint visibility; on a light base, a fingerprint-resistant grade should be specified."

Hot-foil on a soft-touch surface is viable but requires the right paper-foil combination. The softened surface has slightly lower adhesion than gloss laminate. At Huamei, the Heidelberg and KBA press lines hold foil-to-substrate adhesion through press settings and foil selection. If you are specifying large-area foil over soft-touch — a full-logo panel, for example, rather than a small wordmark — confirm the paper and laminate specification before committing to foil tooling.

## Insert sizing for hair care bottles: the three measurements that matter

Hair care bottles are among the least standardised containers in personal care. Craft brands and salon-grade brands especially use non-stock bottle shapes. The three measurements that Huamei needs before cutting any insert blank:

**Body diameter.** The widest point of the bottle body (not the shoulder or the base). This drives the cavity width and determines how much foam wall sits between bottles.

**Total height including cap.** Measured from the base to the top of the closed cap. This drives the internal box height. A box that is 5 mm too short compresses the bottle caps against the lid — a common specification error that appears only when the first physical sample arrives.

**Base profile.** Whether the base is flat, slightly domed, or recessed (like a punt on a glass bottle). A flat-base bottle sits stable in a flat-bottom cavity. A domed base requires a slightly undercut cavity or a raised foam cushion to prevent rocking during transit.

"Huamei cuts every insert blank against three confirmed bottle measurements — body diameter, total height with cap, and base profile — rather than using a generic template, because hair care bottle shapes are rarely standard."

## Certifications and sustainability for hair care brand sourcing

Personal care brands increasingly require sustainability documentation from their packaging suppliers. Huamei holds [FSC](https://fsc.org/) certification on the paper and board supply chain and [BSCI](https://www.bsci-intl.org/) social compliance certification — the two most commonly requested credentials in the personal care sourcing audit. [SGS](https://www.sgs.com/) product testing is available on request for material composition and printing ink compliance.

On the energy side: over 80% of Huamei's factory energy comes from solar generation, with shareholder investment in biomass renewable energy and hydro projects. For personal care brands with Scope 3 emissions reporting or ESG sourcing policies, this is a documentable upstream fact, not a claim that needs further verification.
