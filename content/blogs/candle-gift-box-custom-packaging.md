# Custom candle gift box: structures, finishes, and the production briefing guide

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 8 June 2026. Updated 8 June 2026.

Sonia Sun has run the Huamei press floor since founding the company in Zhengzhou in 1992 — more than three decades producing luxury gift packaging for fragrance, wellness, and seasonal gifting programs across four factories in Henan, Zhejiang, Sichuan, and Guizhou.

A candle gift box is a different brief from a candle retail box. The retail box protects a vessel on a shelf. The gift box has to do more: it must read as a considered object before it opens, hold the vessel securely through last-mile delivery, and give the recipient a reason to keep it after the candle is finished. Structure, surface finish, and insert design determine whether the box earns that second life — or gets recycled the same day.

## What structures suit a custom candle gift box?

A magnetic-flap rigid box in 2.0–2.5 mm greyboard is the standard structure for candle gift sets: it holds the vessel securely, opens with a deliberate tactile motion, and keeps its shape on a shelf. Lift-off lid is used for single-candle retail gifting; sleeve-plus-tray for multi-SKU sets. MOQ from 200 pieces.

The three most-specified structures for candle gifting programs:

**Magnetic-flap rigid box.** Two-piece construction with a hinged lid and embedded magnets. The closure at 6–50 g pull-force (2,800 Gauss) adds a deliberate, tactile open at the moment the recipient first encounters the gift. Standard across prestige fragrance gifting and premium seasonal candle sets. Board weight: 2.0–2.5 mm greyboard. Interior: paper-pulp moulded insert or die-cut card nest cradling the vessel. See [rigid box manufacturing](/craft/rigid) for format comparisons and greyboard specifications.

**Lift-off lid (two-piece tray + lid).** The structurally cleaner option for single-vessel gifting when a magnetic closure is outside budget. The lid lifts straight off; the tray holds the vessel in a die-cut card insert. This format stacks efficiently in warehouse and ships cleanly under FOB terms.

**Sleeve + tray.** The highest-flexibility option for multi-candle gift sets or three-wick products where the vessel diameter varies across SKUs. A printed sleeve slides off a tray insert; the tray accommodates card partitions for two or three candles simultaneously. Useful for holiday gift collections where the same structure runs across multiple fragrance variants.

"A custom candle gift box in 2.0 mm greyboard with a magnetic-flap closure and moulded paper-pulp insert can be produced at Huamei from 200 pieces, with samples in 7–10 days."

## What finishes work on a candle gift box?

Soft-touch laminate paired with a hot-foil logo is the most common finish combination for candle gift packaging: the matte laminate creates a surface the hand reads as premium before the box is opened, and the foil wordmark catches the light when the box is turned under a lamp.

Surface finish options for candle gifting, ranked by cost:

**Soft-touch laminate + hot-foil logo.** The standard prestige combination. Soft-touch over a deep ground — oxblood, forest green, inky navy — reads as restraint rather than decoration. [Seventeen curated hot-foil colours](/craft/hot-foil) in-house, including warm gold and burnished copper used frequently on seasonal candle gifting programs. The [Glees Grove Soaps](/volumes/glees-grove) case study shows a wellness gifting program using this surface register.

**[Soft-touch](/craft/soft-touch) + deboss only.** For sustainability-aligned programs where no metallic finish is wanted. A debossed logo reads as craft without the metallic catch-light. Most common specification for programs targeting ESG-conscious consumers.

**Matte laminate + spot UV.** The spot-UV logo catches the light against the matte field without the cost of a foil die. Suitable for programs where budget does not accommodate hot-foil at the target MOQ.

[FSC](https://fsc.org/en)-certified substrate is available across all finish combinations. Huamei holds FSC chain-of-custody certification, supporting the sustainability sourcing documentation that retail buyers increasingly require.

"Over 80% of Huamei's factory energy comes from solar generation across four provinces — the ESG sourcing data point that US and EU retail procurement teams are increasingly required to document on supplier forms."

## How should a candle gift box insert be specified?

The insert determines whether the vessel survives transit and whether the box reads as well-designed inside. A moulded paper-pulp nest cradling the vessel base is the correct insert for shipping-heavy programs; a die-cut card partition is the most cost-efficient option for retail distribution.

Insert options by use case:

- **Moulded paper-pulp nest** — conforms to the vessel profile; no adhesive foam; [FSC](https://fsc.org/en) compatible. Recommended for direct-to-consumer shipping and any program with a sustainability claim.
- **Die-cut card partition** — holds the vessel with compression fit at lower cost per unit. Suitable for retail distribution where the box does not travel unboxed.
- **Satin or fabric-lined tray** — signals the highest gift tier. Appropriate for prestige seasonal candles or corporate gifting programs where the interior presentation is part of the brand brief.

The [Wild Black Berry wellness case](/volumes/heigouqi) shows a sleeve-plus-tray structure with an insert coordinating both a product vessel and secondary components — the reference for how seasonal gifting programs coordinate interior and exterior.

## What is the production calendar for a seasonal candle gift box?

For a holiday or Christmas launch, candle gift box briefs need to reach the factory by early October to guarantee November in-hands under standard ocean freight terms. Sample production takes 7–10 days; production runs 15–20 days; ocean freight to US ports adds 12–16 days under FOB terms.

Working backward from November 20 in-hands: freight departure November 4; production start October 15; sample approval October 10; brief at factory early October. Programs with multiple SKUs need all variants briefed simultaneously to avoid staggered sample rounds that compress the approval window.

"A candle gift box brief arriving at Huamei by October 1 gives full buffer — samples returned October 10, production run completed November 3, goods shipped by November 4, in-hands by November 20."
