# Thanksgiving corporate gift box: structures, branding, and the briefing calendar from China

By Sonia Sun, Founder, Huamei 華美 — since 1992. Published 5 June 2026. Updated 5 June 2026.

Sonia Sun has produced seasonal gifting packaging at Huamei's four factories in Henan, Zhejiang, Sichuan, and Guizhou since founding the company in 1992, including corporate holiday gift boxes for North American clients sourcing under FOB terms with November delivery requirements.

Thanksgiving corporate gifting occupies a narrow but commercially significant window — gifts must arrive at US offices and distribution points between November 15 and November 28, which means the production and freight calendar works backward from a deadline that moves each year. For corporate procurement teams sourcing from China, this window is tighter than it appears. Ocean freight alone consumes 12–16 transit days. The brief needs to be at the factory by early October at the absolute latest, which means artwork and quantity decisions must be resolved in September. This guide covers the structural and commercial decisions for custom Thanksgiving gift boxes sourced from a China rigid box manufacturer.

## When should corporate buyers brief a Thanksgiving gift box factory in China?

Corporate buyers should brief a Thanksgiving gift box factory in China no later than early October to hit a November delivery window. The 15–20 day production run, plus 12–16 day ocean freight to US ports under FOB terms, means any brief arriving after October 15 risks late arrival.

Working backwards from November 20 as a workable in-hands date: ocean freight from China under [ICC Incoterms 2020](https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/) FOB terms takes 12–16 days to US West Coast ports, so goods must leave China by November 4. Production of 15–20 days means the run must start by October 15 at the latest. Samples — 7–10 days — must be approved before that. The brief must reach the factory by early October, with artwork in the correct format. Corporate programs that require multiple SKUs (different box sizes for different gift tiers) should brief all variants simultaneously to avoid staggered sample rounds.

"A Thanksgiving corporate gift box brief arriving at Huamei by October 1 gives full buffer — samples by October 10, production approved by October 15, goods shipped by November 4, in-hands by November 20."

## What rigid box formats suit corporate Thanksgiving gift packaging?

Two-piece rigid boxes — a tray base and separate lift-off lid — are the most commonly specified format for corporate Thanksgiving gifts because they are structurally clean, photograph well for procurement approvals, and accommodate a range of gift contents including food items, branded merchandise, and curated product sets. The lid carries the corporate logo and seasonal design; the tray holds the insert.

Magnetic-flap rigid boxes suit premium gift tiers — senior client gifts or executive gifting programs — where the opening motion is part of the brand statement. The magnet closure format in 2.5–3.0 mm greyboard signals structural quality without the corporate buyer having to explain it to the recipient. "A corporate Thanksgiving gift box in 2.0 mm greyboard with matte-bronze hot-foil logo and kraft interior can be produced at Huamei from 200 pieces with samples in 7–10 days."

## What branding options work for Thanksgiving corporate gift packaging?

Corporate gift boxes typically carry one design element at high fidelity — the company logo — rather than a complex illustrative treatment. Hot-foil on matte laminate is the most reliable method: it delivers legible, reflective logos on any ground colour and tolerates the wide variety of corporate colour standards. Huamei's [seventeen curated hot-foil colours](/craft/hot-foil) include warm gold, burnished copper, and matte champagne, which all read as autumnal and suit Thanksgiving palette conventions.

Debossed logos without foil are appropriate for corporate programs targeting a sustainability-aligned positioning — they signal craft without the metallic finish. [FSC](https://fsc.org/en)-certified substrate and a deboss-only logo is the most common specification for ESG-conscious corporate gifting programs. The [Man Made Crayon case study](/volumes/man-made-crayon) shows a kraft-finish rigid book-style box with restrained branding, representing the register that sustainability-forward corporate gift programs typically target.

"Over 80% of Huamei's factory energy comes from solar generation, which supports the ESG sourcing narrative that US corporate procurement teams are increasingly expected to document."

## What interior specification should corporate Thanksgiving boxes carry?

Corporate gift box inserts serve containment and presentation functions simultaneously. The simplest option is a card partition set dividing the tray into cells for multiple gift items. Paper-pulp moulded inserts are appropriate where [FSC](https://fsc.org/en) chain-of-custody is required. Satin or fabric-lined tray bases add a premium feel for high-value gift tiers. The [Glees Grove Soaps case study](/volumes/glees-grove) shows a wellness-category packaging program with a floral folding carton, illustrating the insert-plus-outer coordination that corporate gifting programs need across a curated product set.

## How does Huamei's transit testing protect corporate gift deliveries?

Corporate gifts that ship from China to US distribution centres or directly to office addresses travel by ocean freight and last-mile carrier. Huamei's transit testing covers high 50 °C and low −30 °C environmental simulation, 24-hour vibration, drop, and empty-box compression — the full export-grade protocol. For corporate gifting programs where presentation quality on arrival is part of the brand brief, transit testing confirmation is a standard procurement requirement.
